# Statistics & Sampling - Practice Quiz
## Q&A
### 1. Which measure of central tendency is most affected by extreme values?
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Mean

### 2. Standard deviation is the square root of
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Variance

### 3. In stratified sampling, the population is first divided into
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Homogeneous subgroups called strata

### 4. Which sampling method gives every member of the population an equal chance of selection?
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Simple random sampling

### 5. Find the median of: 3, 7, 9, 12, 15
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** 9

### 6. If P(A) = 0.5 and P(B) = 0.4 for independent events, then P(A and B) equals
* (confidence 0.90)
**Answer:** 2

### 7. A Type I error in hypothesis testing is
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Rejecting a true null hypothesis

### 8. The Karl Pearson correlation coefficient (r) always lies between
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** -1 and +1

### 9. Which of the following is a measure of dispersion?
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Standard deviation

* (confidence 0.99)
