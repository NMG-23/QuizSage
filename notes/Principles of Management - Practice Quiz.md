# Principles of Management - Practice Quiz
## Q&A
### 1. Who is known as the father of scientific management?
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** W. Taylor

### 2. Fayol's principle of 'unity of command' means
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Each employee receives orders from one superior only

### 3. How many principles of management did Henri Fayol propose?
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** 14

### 4. Planning, as a function of management, is primarily concerned with
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Deciding in advance what to do and how to do it

### 5. Span of control refers to
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** The number of subordinates a manager can effectively supervise

### 6. The hierarchy of needs theory of motivation was proposed by
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Abraham Maslow

### 7. Decentralization of authority means
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Delegating authority to lower levels

### 8. The Hawthorne experiments, which led to the human relations movement, were conducted under the guidance of
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Elton Mayo

### 9. The controlling function of management involves
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Comparing actual performance with standards and taking corrective action

### 10. Fayol's principle 'esprit de corps' emphasizes
* (confidence 0.99)
**Answer:** Unity and team spirit among employees
